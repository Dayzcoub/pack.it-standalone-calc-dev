## Privacy Policy — PACK.IT Calculators

Effective date: TBD

PACK.IT Calculators is designed as an offline-first technical calculator app for stage, truss and LED screen calculations.

## 1. Data collection

In version 1.0, the app is intended not to collect personal data.

The app does not require:

- account registration;
- email address;
- phone number;
- user name;
- location;
- contacts;
- camera;
- microphone.

## 2. Local data storage

The app may store the following data locally on your device:

- saved calculations;
- calculation drafts;
- price profile settings;
- theme and app settings;
- generated PDF files if you choose to save them.

This data is stored on your device and is not sent to PACK.IT servers in version 1.0.

## 3. PDF export and sharing

PDF files are generated locally on your device.

When you use the Share function, your device operating system opens the native share sheet. You choose where to send the file. PACK.IT Calculators does not control third-party apps selected by you in the share sheet.

## 4. Internet connection

Version 1.0 is intended to work offline.

If future versions add cloud sync, accounts, analytics, crash reporting or other online features, this policy must be updated before release.

## 5. Analytics and tracking

Version 1.0 is intended not to include analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking SDKs.

## 6. Data deletion

You can delete saved calculations inside the app.

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored app data from your device, depending on the operating system behavior.

## 7. Safety note

Calculations are for reference only and do not replace engineering verification, equipment documentation, manufacturer requirements, safety regulations or the responsibility of a qualified specialist on site.
