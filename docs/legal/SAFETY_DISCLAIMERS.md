# Safety Disclaimers

## Purpose

PACK.IT produces reference planning outputs.

It is not an engineering certificate and not a safety approval tool.

## Required wording direction

Use wording such as:

- reference calculation
- requires verification
- check manufacturer documentation
- check equipment passports
- qualified specialist must verify on site

## Forbidden claims

Avoid claims such as:

- safe
- certified
- guaranteed
- will hold
- approved for mounting

## PDF

PDF documents must include safety and responsibility disclaimer text.

## Task 001

Task 001 should keep a placeholder for legal copy and PDF disclaimer source.
