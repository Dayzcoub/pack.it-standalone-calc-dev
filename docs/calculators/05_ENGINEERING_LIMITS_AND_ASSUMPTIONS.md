# 05. Engineering Limits and Assumptions

## 1. Purpose

PACK.IT calculations are reference calculations, not certified engineering approvals.

This document defines what the calculator does and does not claim.

## 2. General limits

The app does not replace:

- structural engineering calculation;
- equipment passport data;
- manufacturer manuals;
- local safety standards;
- qualified specialist responsibility;
- site inspection.

## 3. Stage assumptions

Stage calculator may assume:

- selected stage system matches catalog data;
- floor/ground capacity is not checked;
- wind/outdoor factors are not checked;
- dynamic loads are not fully calculated;
- custom local regulations are not applied.

## 4. Truss assumptions

Truss calculator may assume:

- catalog dimensions/weights are correct;
- max unsupported span rule is a product safety heuristic;
- load check is based only on entered values;
- rigging points, roof structure and site conditions are not certified;
- wind and dynamic loads are not fully calculated;
- manufacturer load tables must be checked.

## 5. LED assumptions

LED calculator may assume:

- cabinet/module power and weight are correct as entered;
- electrical distribution is not fully engineered;
- rigging/standing safety must be verified;
- custom cabinet values are user-defined and require checking.

## 6. Warning language

Use cautious language:

```text
по введённым данным
справочно
требует проверки
проверьте паспорт оборудования
```

Avoid:

```text
гарантированно безопасно
разрешено к монтажу
сертифицировано
```

## 7. PDF requirement

PDF must include disclaimer and important warnings.

## 8. Acceptance

Accepted when:

- disclaimer appears in app/PDF;
- load/safety wording is cautious;
- custom/user-defined data is marked;
- no UI says a construction is legally approved.
