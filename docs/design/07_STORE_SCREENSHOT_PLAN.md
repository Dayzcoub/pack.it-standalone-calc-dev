# 07. Store Screenshot Plan

## 1. Goal

Store screenshots must explain the product quickly and look consistent with the approved PACK.IT visual direction.

They are marketing assets, not raw debug screenshots.

## 3. Visual requirements

- dark theme preferred;
- phone mockup allowed;
- captions should be large and readable;
- no fake impossible data;
- no FEG brand;
- no debug UI;
- no placeholder icons.

## 4. App Store / Google Play notes

Prepare separate sizes later according to the actual store requirements.

Keep original mockup source files and export-ready PNGs.

## 5. Acceptance

Store screenshots are accepted when:

- they match actual implemented UI;
- captions are correct;
- screenshots are readable at store preview size;
- all three calculators are represented;
- PDF/export value is visible;
- no old brand remains.
