# Pricing Engine

## Purpose

Pricing Engine calculates project price from ProjectModel and SceneModel.

## Rule

Pricing is generated from scene objects and project settings.

Do not calculate final price from isolated calculator forms.

## MVP direction

- local price profile;
- optional price visibility;
- generated price snapshot;
- PDF can include or hide prices.

## Future

- multiple local price profiles;
- import/export of price settings;
- catalog-linked price rows.
